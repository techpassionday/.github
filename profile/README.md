# Tech Passion Day

Tech Passion Day is about one thing: **people who genuinely love technology sharing that passion with others**.

It is a community-driven technology conference where developers, architects, technology leaders, builders, and curious minds come together to explore what is happening in tech right now — and what might be coming next.

Expect deep technical sessions, new ideas, practical experiences, experiments, demos, lessons learned, and occasionally things that probably should not work... but somehow do.

We cover everything from software engineering, cloud, architecture, security, and data to AI, agents, developer tools, and emerging technologies.

## What you’ll find here

This is the home for material from the Tech Passion Day conferences.

Across the repositories you will find things like:

- Slides from the sessions
- Demo applications and source code
- Samples and experiments
- Links, tools, and additional resources
- Supporting material shared by the speakers

Some sessions may have a complete demo project. Others might contain just a few slides or useful links. That is part of the idea — this is the material behind the talks, shared as openly and practically as possible.

## Keep exploring

A conference session might last 45 minutes, but hopefully the curiosity lasts a lot longer.

Use the material here to revisit a session, run the demos, break something, change something, build something new, or simply dive deeper into a technology you discovered at Tech Passion Day.

Because technology keeps changing.

And that is exactly what makes it fun.

**Stay curious. Keep experimenting. Share what you learn.**
