# Tech Passion Day

Tech Passion Day is a community-focused technology conference for people who are curious, passionate, and excited about technology.

The event brings together developers, architects, technology leaders, and other tech enthusiasts to share knowledge, explore new ideas, and learn from real-world experiences. The sessions cover a broad range of topics across modern software development, cloud, AI, architecture, security, data, and emerging technologies.

This repository is part of the **Tech Passion Day** community and is where we collect and share material from the conferences.

Depending on the individual sessions, you may find:

- Presentation slides
- Demo applications and source code
- Samples and examples
- Links and additional resources
- Supporting material from speakers

The goal is to make the knowledge shared at Tech Passion Day available beyond the conference itself, so you can revisit sessions, experiment with the demos, and continue exploring the technologies and ideas presented.

## About the event

Tech Passion Day is built around a simple idea: **passion for technology is worth sharing**.

Technology changes quickly, but curiosity, experimentation, and learning from each other remain some of the most important skills we have. Tech Passion Day creates a place for exactly that — bringing people together to learn, share experiences, challenge existing thinking, and discover what might be next.
